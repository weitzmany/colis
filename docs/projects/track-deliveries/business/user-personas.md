# User Personas

## Primary Persona: Sarah (Frequent Online Shopper)
- **Age**: 28-45
- **Behavior**: Orders weekly from multiple retailers
- **Needs**: Unified tracking, proactive alerts
- **Pain Points**: Fragmented tracking across apps

## Primary Persona: Michael (Multi-Household Manager)
- **Age**: 35-50
- **Behavior**: Tracks household and family deliveries
- **Needs**: Organization by recipient, alerts for signature-required items
- **Pain Points**: Confusion over whose package arrives when

## Secondary Persona: Jessica (Power User)
- **Age**: 22-35
- **Behavior**: International shopping, long delivery windows
- **Needs**: Long-term tracking, customs updates
- **Pain Points**: International tracking complexity
