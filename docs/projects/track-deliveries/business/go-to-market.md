# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Launch Channels
1. Product Hunt
2. Reddit communities (with moderator approval)
3. Social media (Twitter, Instagram)
4. Content marketing (delivery tips, carrier comparisons)

## Acquisition Targets
- 100 users in month 1
- CAC under $10 per user

## Retention Strategy
- Welcome email with tutorial
- Weekly delivery digest
- Reactivation email when new package is added

## Positioning
- Privacy-first, ad-free delivery tracking
- Unified timeline with proactive alerts
- Affordable premium tier
