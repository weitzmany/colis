# Business Model

## Overview
Track Deliveries uses a freemium subscription model with a free tier for casual users and a premium tier for power users.

## Pricing
- **Free Tier**: 10 active packages, email alerts, 30-day history
- **Premium Tier**: $4.99/month or $49.99/year

## Premium Benefits
- Unlimited packages
- Advanced alerts (push in Phase 2, SMS in Phase 3)
- Full delivery history
- Export reports (CSV/PDF)
- Priority support

## Revenue Projections (Year 1)
- **Users**: 1,100 total (1,000 free + 100 premium)
- **MRR**: $499
- **ARR**: ~$6,000

## Assumptions
- 5-10% conversion from free to premium
- Average 3-5 active packages per user

## Risks
- Conversion rate below target
- Increased carrier API costs
