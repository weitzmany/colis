# Business: Go-to-Market

## Target Segments
- Families coordinating elder care
- Parents managing multiple caregivers
- Professional caregivers supporting family care

## Acquisition Channels
- Search content on caregiver coordination
- Partnerships with care organizations
- Referral incentives within the product

## Launch Plan
- Private beta with 20 families
- Public MVP launch after 2 weeks of feedback
- Iterative feature releases every 3 to 4 weeks
