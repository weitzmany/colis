# Business: Revenue Model

## Overview
Freemium subscription model with a free tier for basic coordination and a premium tier for advanced capabilities.

## Free Tier
- 1 dependent profile
- Shared calendar and tasks
- Limited document storage

## Premium Tier
- Multiple dependents
- Advanced reminders and routines
- Unlimited document storage
- Care team roles and permissions

## Pricing Notes
- Target range: $9 to $15 per month
- Family bundle pricing encouraged for multi-dependent households
