# AI Logo Generator - IP and Licensing Compliance

## Copyright and Ownership
- Terms of service should clarify logo ownership for users
- Generated logos should be treated as user-owned assets

## Risk Mitigations
- Use AI models trained on licensed or public domain datasets
- Implement similarity checking to reduce copying risk
- Provide clear refund policy if a logo conflicts with existing trademarks

## Post-MVP Enhancements
- Offer optional copyright guarantee or insurance
- Provide guidance on trademark registration for paid users
