# Compliance: Privacy and Data Protection

## Data Categories
- Personal profile data for caregivers
- Dependent care details and routines
- Medical and emergency information
- Documents with sensitive content

## Privacy Principles
- Collect only what is needed for coordination
- Provide clear consent for document sharing
- Allow data export and deletion by family admins

## Data Retention
- Retain audit logs for 2 years
- Allow families to delete documents at any time

## Security Controls
- Encryption at rest and in transit
- Role-based access enforcement
- Reviewable audit logs for sensitive actions
