# Colis Company Showcase

**Status**: Planning
**Priority**: High
**Category**: Full-Stack Web Application

## Overview

Colis is a comprehensive company showcase website designed for client acquisition and portfolio presentation. The platform presents the company's services, project portfolio, team members, and expertise to potential clients.

## Key Features

- **Company Overview**: Professional company presentation with history and values
- **Project Portfolio**: Showcase all company projects with case studies
- **Team Profiles**: Staff member profiles with expertise and roles
- **Services**: Detailed service offerings and capabilities
- **Blog/News**: Content management for company updates and insights
- **Client Testimonials**: Social proof and success stories
- **CMS**: Content management system for easy updates

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Angular
- **Backend**: Slim PHP 4
- **Database**: MySQL/SQLite
- **CMS**: Custom CMS for content management
- **Hosting**: TBD

## Primary Purpose

- Client acquisition and marketing
- Portfolio showcase for potential clients
- Professional company presence

## Target Users

- Potential clients seeking development services
- Business decision-makers evaluating partners
- Stakeholders interested in company capabilities

## Content Integration

- Integration with existing project documentation
- Automated project showcase generation
- Dynamic content updates from docs/projects/

## Business Value

- Generate qualified leads
- Professional brand presence
- Showcase technical expertise
- Build client trust through transparency
- Demonstrate successful project delivery
