# Revenue Model - Recipes

## Business Model Overview

**Recipes** uses a **freemium model** with a generous free tier to drive user adoption and a premium tier ($7.99/month or $79.99/year) that unlocks advanced features for power users.

### Revenue Streams

1. **Premium Subscriptions** (Primary) - 90% of revenue
2. **Affiliate Commissions** (Future) - 5-10% of revenue
3. **B2B Licensing** (Future) - Potential revenue stream

---

## Pricing Tiers

### Free Tier

**Target**: Casual home cooks, trial users, price-sensitive users

**Features**:
- ✅ Save up to 100 recipes
- ✅ Basic meal planning (7 days)
- ✅ Up to 3 recipe collections
- ✅ Cooking mode with timers
- ✅ Mobile-responsive web app (PWA)
- ✅ Recipe search and filtering
- ✅ Tag recipes

**Limitations**:
- ❌ Recipe limit: 100 recipes maximum
- ❌ Collection limit: 3 collections maximum
- ❌ Meal planning: 7 days only (no extended planning)
- ❌ No recipe import from URLs (manual entry only)
- ❌ No auto-generated grocery lists
- ❌ No nutrition information
- ❌ No native mobile app access

**Conversion Strategy**:
- Introduce soft paywall when approaching limits
- Highlight premium features in UI (with upgrade prompts)
- 14-day free trial of premium tier (no credit card required)

---

### Premium Tier

**Price**: $7.99/month or $79.99/year (17% savings)

**Target**: Dedicated home cooks, meal planners, health-conscious users

**Features**:
- ✅ **Unlimited recipes** - Save as many recipes as you want
- ✅ **Unlimited collections** - Organize with unlimited collections
- ✅ **Advanced meal planning** - Plan up to 30 days ahead
- ✅ **Auto-generated grocery lists** - Automatic shopping lists from meal plans
- ✅ **Recipe import from URLs** - Import recipes from any website
- ✅ **Nutrition insights** - Calorie, protein, carb tracking (Phase 3)
- ✅ **Native mobile app access** - iOS and Android apps (Phase 2)
- ✅ **Dietary filters** - Filter by diet (vegan, gluten-free, etc.) (Phase 3)
- ✅ **Priority support** - Email support with <24hr response time
- ✅ **Ad-free experience** - No ads or sponsored content
- ✅ **Early access** - New features before free users

**Value Proposition**:
- "Unlimited recipes and smart meal planning for serious home cooks"
- "Save $16/year with annual subscription"

**Target Conversion Rate**: 8% of active users

## Pricing Strategy

### Competitive Analysis

| Competitor | Free Tier | Premium Price | Key Differences |
|------------|-----------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Paprika** | N/A | $4.99-$19.99 (one-time) | One-time payment, fewer features, no cloud sync |
| **Mealime** | Yes | $5.99/month | Similar features, higher price, smaller recipe database |
| **Yummly** | Yes | $4.99/month | Large user base, ad-heavy free tier, simpler features |
| **Recipes** | Yes (100 recipes) | $7.99/month | Mobile-first, generous free tier, premium features |

### Our Positioning

**Why $7.99/month?**
1. **Higher perceived value** than $4.99 competitors
2. **More features** than one-time purchase apps
3. **Sustainable pricing** for ongoing development
4. **Premium positioning** signals quality

**Why 17% annual discount?**
1. **Industry standard** (most SaaS offer 15-20% annual discount)
2. **Increases LTV** (Lifetime Value) by encouraging annual subscriptions
3. **Reduces churn** (users less likely to cancel annual plans)

**Elasticity Assumptions**:
- **$7.99/month**: 8% conversion rate (baseline)
- **$9.99/month**: 6% conversion rate (too expensive for casual users)
- **$5.99/month**: 10% conversion rate (but 25% less revenue per user)

**Decision**: $7.99/month maximizes revenue while maintaining healthy conversion rate.

## Revenue Optimization Strategies

### 1. Free Tier Optimization

**Goal**: Maximize signups and engagement while driving premium conversions

**Tactics**:
- **Generous limits**: 100 recipes is enough to be useful but encourages upgrade for serious cooks
- **Highlight premium**: Show premium features in UI with clear upgrade prompts
- **Soft paywalls**: "Upgrade to save more recipes" when approaching limit
- **14-day trial**: Risk-free premium trial (no credit card required)

**Success Metrics**:
- 70%+ of signups save at least 5 recipes
- 30%+ of users reach 50 recipe limit (ready to convert)
- 10%+ of users hit 100 recipe limit (highest conversion intent)

### 2. Premium Conversion Optimization

**Goal**: Convert 8% of active users to premium

**Tactics**:
- **Value-based messaging**: "Meal planning saves you 30+ minutes per week"
- **Social proof**: "Join 800+ home cooks who upgraded to premium"
- **Urgency**: Limited-time offer for new users (20% off first month)
- **Feature discovery**: Show premium features in context (e.g., "Upgrade to import this recipe")

**Conversion Triggers**:
- User hits recipe limit (100 recipes)
- User creates 5+ meal plans (shows meal planning value)
- User uses cooking mode 10+ times (engaged user)
- User returns 3+ weeks in a row (habit formation)

### 3. Retention & Churn Reduction

**Goal**: Reduce churn to <5% monthly

**Tactics**:
- **Engagement emails**: Weekly email with meal planning tips, new recipes
- **Win-back campaigns**: Re-engage users who haven't logged in for 2 weeks
- **Cancellation surveys**: Understand why users cancel, offer discounts/solutions
- **Annual discounts**: Encourage annual subscriptions (lower churn, higher LTV)

**Retention Metrics**:
- 3-month retention: 60%+
- 6-month retention: 40%+
- 12-month retention: 25%+

### 4. Lifetime Value (LTV) Maximization

**Goal**: Increase LTV to $120+ (15 months average subscription)

**Tactics**:
- **Annual subscriptions**: 17% discount encourages 12-month commitment
- **Feature expansion**: Add premium features over time (nutrition, voice commands)
- **Product bundling**: Bundle with meal kit services or grocery delivery (Phase 3)

**LTV Calculation**:
```
LTV = Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) × Average Customer Lifetime

Monthly: $7.99 × 15 months = $119.85
Annual: $79.99 × 1.5 years = $119.99
```

**Target LTV**: $120 (15-month average subscription)

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

### Acquisition Channels

**Organic (Months 1-6)**:
- Product Hunt launch
- Content marketing (recipe blogs, meal planning guides)
- SEO (recipe searches)
- Social media (Instagram, TikTok cooking videos)
- Referral program (invite friends, earn premium features)

**Paid (Months 6+)**:
- Facebook/Instagram ads targeting home cooks
- Google Ads (recipe, meal planning keywords)
- Influencer partnerships (food bloggers, home cook influencers)

### CAC Targets

| Channel | CAC Target | Conversion Rate | Notes |
|---------|------------|-----------------|-------|
| Organic (SEO, social) | $5-10 | 10% | Low CAC, scalable |
| Content marketing | $8-12 | 8% | Blog posts, guides |
| Referrals | $3-5 | 12% | Existing users invite friends |
| Paid ads (Facebook) | $15-25 | 5% | Higher CAC, targeted |

## Key Business Metrics

### Primary Metrics (Track Weekly)

1. **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: Total subscription revenue per month
   - **Target**: $6,400 by Month 12, $20,000 by Month 24

2. **Active Users**: Users who log in at least once per month
   - **Target**: 10,000 by Month 12, 30,000 by Month 24

3. **Premium Conversion Rate**: % of active users who subscribe to premium
   - **Target**: 8% conversion rate

4. **Churn Rate**: % of premium users who cancel per month
   - **Target**: <5% monthly churn

5. **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Cost to acquire one new user
   - **Target**: $10-15 per user (blended)

### Secondary Metrics (Track Monthly)

6. **Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Average revenue per user over their lifetime
   - **Target**: $120 (15-month average subscription)

7. **LTV:CAC Ratio**: Ratio of lifetime value to acquisition cost
   - **Target**: >6:1 (healthy SaaS target: >3:1)

8. **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)**: Total revenue / total users
   - **Target**: $0.80-1.00 ARPU (includes free + premium users)

9. **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**: Likelihood to recommend (0-10 scale)
   - **Target**: NPS >50 (promoters - detractors)

10. **Recipe Saves Per User**: Average recipes saved per user
    - **Target**: 25 recipes per user

## Pricing Experiments & A/B Tests

### Planned Experiments (Year 1)

**1. Annual Discount Test** (Month 6)
- **Hypothesis**: 20% discount increases annual subscriptions by 30%+
- **Variants**: 17% off (control), 20% off (test), 25% off (test 2)
- **Metric**: % of users choosing annual vs monthly

**2. Free Trial Length** (Month 4)
- **Hypothesis**: 14-day trial converts better than 7-day trial
- **Variants**: 7 days (control), 14 days (test), 30 days (test 2)
- **Metric**: Trial-to-paid conversion rate

**3. Feature Gating** (Month 8)
- **Hypothesis**: Showing premium features earlier increases conversions
- **Variants**: Gate at 50 recipes (control), gate at 25 recipes (test)
- **Metric**: Conversion rate, user satisfaction

**4. Pricing Test** (Month 12)
- **Hypothesis**: $7.99 is optimal price point for conversions
- **Variants**: $5.99 (test), $7.99 (control), $9.99 (test 2)
- **Metric**: Conversion rate × price (maximize revenue)
